Small denominators produce unstable rates: one or two removals can swing a rate a great deal (Hullman 2020). The slider compares each measure with its own denominator (all students, Black students, or White students; the smaller of the two for the risk ratio). Hidden districts turn grey.

Data: Texas Education Agency, Annual District Discipline Summary (PEIMS). Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic school districts (2023), joined through the NCES CCD crosswalk.

Definitions
  • Discretionary removals per 100 students = (in-school suspension actions + out-of-school suspension actions + discretionary DAEP placement actions) ÷ cumulative year-end enrollment × 100 (TEA section B, “Action counts”; section A, “Cumulative year end enrollment”). One student can receive several actions, so this rate can exceed 100.
  • Black / White students: removals per 100 students of that group = the group’s ISS + OSS + discretionary DAEP actions (TEA sections C, D, E) ÷ the group’s cumulative enrollment (section A) × 100.
  • Black-White risk ratio = Black rate ÷ White rate. Values above 1 mean Black students are removed at a higher rate than White students in the same district; undefined when the White rate is 0.
  • Students with an in-/out-of-school suspension (% of enrolled) = students suspended at least once (section B, “Student counts”) ÷ enrollment × 100.
  • Discretionary DAEP removals per 1,000 students = discretionary DAEP placement actions ÷ enrollment × 1,000.
  • Grey districts: TEA masked a value to protect student privacy (shown as “<10”, “<20”, etc.), the district has no TEA match, or the denominator is below the slider threshold. TEA prints no row when a category is zero, so absent rows are counted as 0.
  • Open-enrollment charter schools have no geographic boundary, so they cannot appear on the map.
